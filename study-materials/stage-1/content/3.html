The Bench Routine

Measure carefully. Join cleanly. Leave the bench safer than you found it. Every connection has a check:

  1. Measure before touching the circuit.
  2. Join the parts with heat and solder.
  3. Protect the finished connection.

Use the Multimeter Without Guessing

Use the multimeter without guessing (YouTube)

Choose the function and the jack before the probes touch the circuit.

Bench Routine

  1. Choose — Voltage, current, resistance, or continuity.
  2. Connect — Black probe in COM; red probe in VΩ or A/mA.
  3. Read — Touch carefully and watch the display.
  4. Reset — Remove power before changing connections.
Warning

Current uses the A/mA jack and must be measured in series — never like voltage.

Soldering Joins the Component to the PCB

Soldering joins the component to the PCB (YouTube)

Heat the pad and lead together, then let the solder flow into the joint.

  1. Touch the iron to the pad and component lead.
  2. Feed solder into the heated joint.
  3. Pull solder away first.
  4. Lift the iron and let the joint cool.

Good Joint

Stop and fix

Avoid: bridges, blobs, dull cracked joints, and lifted pads.

Inspect before power.

Desoldering Removes a Connection Cleanly

Desoldering removes a connection cleanly (YouTube)

Free a component or correct a soldering mistake without damaging the pad or board.

Pump

  1. Heat the joint.
  2. Position the nozzle.
  3. Press the release and pull molten solder away.

Wick

  1. Place braid on the joint.
  2. Heat through the copper braid.
  3. Lift the braid after it absorbs solder.
Basic rule

Use brief heat, keep the tip clean, and stop if the pad starts to lift.

Final Bench Check

Workshop safety is part of every connection.

Protect

Wear eye protection. Keep loose clothing, hair, and cables away from the iron.

Park

Rest the hot iron in its stand. Never leave it tip-down or unattended.

Ventilate

Work with ventilation, wash hands after soldering, and keep food and drink away.

Power off

Disconnect power before changing probe leads, desoldering, or moving a circuit.

Check the meter

Confirm the range and jack. Never measure current like voltage.

Reset the bench

Iron parked, power off, scraps cleared, tools returned.

Workshop Tool 01 — Angle Grinder

Cut, grind, and clean metal edges.

Purpose

Remove excess metal, cut stock, and smooth welds.

How to use

Fit the correct disc, hold the grinder with two hands, start it clear of the work, then guide it steadily through the cut or across the surface.

Safety

Wear eye and face protection, hearing protection, gloves, and closed footwear. Check the guard and disc for damage. Keep sparks away from people, cables, and flammables.

Workshop Tool 02 — Drill Machine

Make accurate holes safely.

Purpose

Drill holes for fasteners, brackets, and cable routes.

How to use

Mark and center-punch the hole, clamp the work securely, select the bit and speed, then apply steady pressure without forcing the tool.

Safety

Wear eye protection and tie back long hair or loose clothing. Remove the chuck key before starting. Never hold small work by hand, and wait for the bit to stop before clearing swarf.
